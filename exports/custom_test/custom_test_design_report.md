- **Engineering Rationale:** Link distance of 80.0m is within the 100-meter electrical channel limit specified by IEEE 802.3an. 10GBASE-T over Cat6a delivers 10 Gbps throughput at 500 MHz bandwidth with superior power efficiency and eliminates costly optical transceivers for intra-building runs.

### Link 2: Core-Switch -> HR-Block

- **Engineering Rationale:** Link distance of 250.0m exceeds the 100m copper limit but sits safely within the 400m operating range of OM4 laser-optimized MMF. OM4 provides an effective modal bandwidth of 4700 MHz·km at 850nm VCSEL transmission, avoiding chromatic dispersion while lowering optics cost.

### Link 3: HQ -> Remote-Office
- **Calculated Distance:** `15000.0 meters`

- **Transceiver Module:** `SFP-10G-ER (1550nm, up to 40km)` (1550 nm)
- **Standard:** `IEEE 802.3ae (10GBASE-LR/ER)`
- **Engineering Rationale:** Link distance of 15000.0m exceeds the 400m dispersion envelope of multimode fiber. OS2 single-mode fiber with a 9µm core eliminates intermodal dispersion entirely, providing an optical attenuation budget <= 0.4 dB/km at 1550nm for metropolitan/WAN spans.

## 4. L3 Dynamic Routing & Policy Architecture

- **Primary Routing Protocol:** Single-Area OSPFv2 (RFC 2328)
- **Backbone Topology Tier:** Collapsed Core / Campus Enterprise Backbone
- **Area Assignment:** Area 0 (Backbone)
- **Border Interconnect:** None (Self-Contained AS)
- **Protocol Rationale:** Topology scale (4 subnets within a single autonomous campus) does not justify the Link-State Database (LSDB) SPF computational overhead of ABR summarization boundaries. Single-Area OSPF Area 0 guarantees microsecond Dijkstra convergence, simplifies zero-touch adjacencies, and complies with RFC 2328 campus best practices.
